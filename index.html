                Welcome aboard

                Fasten your focus.

                Cruising Altitude turns study sessions into flights. Pick how long you want to focus, choose a destination that's about that far away, and fly there while you work.

                Where's home?

                Your first flights leave from here. After each landing, you can keep going from wherever you are, and slowly work your way around the world.

                A daily goal

                How much focus feels like a good day? Reach it to fill the day's ring and grow your streak, which also upgrades your seat class.

                How a flight works

                1. Choose a length with the slider, or plan a trip with breaks.
                2. Pick a destination and fill in what you're working on.
                3. Slide to board, watch the takeoff, then focus while your plane crosses the globe.
                4. Land to earn passport stamps, city badges, new planes and liveries.

                Sound and feel

                You can change any of these later in Settings.
